## TONE & STYLE NOTES

✅ **DO:**
- Use conversational, relatable language
- Focus on **pain points** first, then solutions
- Show real interface (not just animations)
- Include subtle humor (but keep it professional)
- Emphasize time-saving and ease-of-use

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use overly technical jargon
- Show cluttered or confusing screens
- Go too fast (let people see what's happening)
- Make wild claims without evidence
- Forget to show the human benefit

---

## SCRIPT TIPS FOR NARRATOR

1. **Energy**: Start moderate, build energy through the middle, peak at CTA
2. **Pacing**: Slow on problem, moderate on features, fast on benefits
3. **Emphasis**: Stress action words ("seamlessly," "instantly," "effortlessly")
4. **Confidence**: Speak like you believe this solves real problems
5. **Relatability**: Use "you" and "your" to make it personal
